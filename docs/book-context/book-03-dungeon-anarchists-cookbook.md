# Dungeon Crawler Carl, Book 3: The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook

Reference summary for the side-scroller team. Source: `DCC_03.md` (about 9,990 lines). Line numbers in brackets (like [L1200]) are approximate positions in that file. Everything here is paraphrased. Where something is uncertain, it says so.

## 1. Overview and plot summary

**Overview.** Book 3 covers the entire **fourth floor, "The Iron Tangle,"** a deliberately confusing, puzzle-box level built from a planet-sized subway/railway network. The crawl timer is only **10 days** (normally 20), because the AI is lashing out after the third-floor veto. The floor swaps the explosive, town-based fantasy of floor 3 for tight, cramped, close-quarters fighting inside train cars, tunnels, platforms and trainyards, with a heavy "evil transit corporation" satire (human resources, overtime, drugs, a political-cartoon plot against the Krakaren). Carl, Princess Donut and Mongo are joined by **Katia Grim** (a doppelganger/shapeshifter who becomes a tank) and, after a time-out punishment, lose their manager **Mordecai** for seven days.

**What carries over.** Carl (Compensated Anarchist, explosives, invulnerable feet via pedicure kit, Protective Shell, xistera, gauntlet), Donut (spell caster, Second Chance, Clockwork Triplicate, Puddle Jumper, Cockroach survival), Mongo the raptor pet, Mordecai the game guide (now a toad), Zev the PR agent, Odette the interviewer, the Meadow Lark survivors (Imani, Mrs. McGibbons), Bautista, Hekla and Brynhild's Daughters, Quan Ch's Celestial jacket, Valtay vs. Borant politics, the Skull Empire family (Prince Maestro, Prince Stalwart, the war god Grull), the nuke "Carl's Doomsday Scenario," the Desperado Club.

**What changes.** New class/race rules (temporary classes can leave permanent skills), a personal-space upgrade system, bounties on the top 10, sponsors for every crawler, a crawler-versus-crawler (PVP) coupon system, third-party Valtay implants, and the secret book **The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook**, which Carl wins from a fan-box game show and which feeds him lore and recipes for the rest of the series.

**Plot in one paragraph (spoilers).** Carl, Donut, Mongo and Katia drop into a Moscow-style Metro train full of drug-addled monsters, survive using a stationary Protective Shell, and reach Transfer Station 83. The AI's coupons let Donut talk a Bopca into a giant, shareable base. Brandon (from Meadow Lark) is confirmed dead and Carl starts learning the floor: a one-way web of colored subway lines (monsters ride between "every fifth stop" rooms where they get a drug fix) plus named loop trains. Mordecai is punished with a 7-day time-out after attacking Chaco, the host of Carl's fan-box show; Carl's chosen prize is the Cookbook. Carl derails a train, kills the ManTauR engineer Gore-Gore, gets sponsors (Valtay for Carl, Princess D'nadia for Donut, Princess Formidable for Katia), steals the cursed steam train "Nightmare Express," and discovers the secret: the souvenir hat everyone got at the start is a key that lets you ride the lines through portals past the Abyss to the trainyards. Withdrawing monsters turn into ghouls and eventually Krakaren clones. Hekla tries to murder Katia to break Carl's party; Katia accidentally kills Hekla, takes her legendary crossbow, and Brynhild's Daughters collapse. Carl rescues 1,400 stranded crawlers using portal-blade rail carts powered by a repeatedly murdered, regenerating gnoll bartender, then realizes the Krakaren will pour through trapdoors in the stairwell rooms and plugs the hole with a hanging portal cart. He tricks the mimic boss into a showdown with the war god Grull (piloted by the Maestro, who is alive), survives, destroys the system with a bomb-laden train, and descends. In the epilogue the AI/network replaces Zev with a hostile new PR agent (Loita), and the party spins a wheel that sends them to Floor 5's Air quadrant: the Dirigible Gnomes' sky fortress.

## 4. World and System rules (new or changed)

- **Floor timer 10 days; no early stairs.** Stairwells open only six hours before collapse. 9,375 stairwells exist; they are located at stations 12, 24, 36, 48, 72 on any line (plus 433).
- **Transfer stations** have prime numbers; saferooms and shops appear only there. Other stations are cave nests.
- **Colored lines** are one-way, rising in number; **named trains** (Nightmare Express, Dismemberment Limited, Escape Velocity, Eviscerator) loop and are more dangerous. Every fifth stop (ending 5 or 0) is where monsters exit to a "fix" room.
- **Engineer keys and souvenir hat** are portal keys. Equipping the starting hat (or using an engineer key) lets you pass the Abyss portals.
- **Personal spaces**: coupons give Level-3 spaces which can be conjoined (3+3+3 = Level 5). A manager gets his own room at Level 5. Environmental upgrades: ultra bed (2-hour sleep, Good Rest +10% stats/xp for 30h), shower (+10% stats, lower detectability), training room (one non-spell skill per day), crafting studio, later a cleaner robot. Tables upgrade on floor change.
- **Bounties and sponsors**: top-10 crawlers have bounties; survivors get 10% on collapse. Every crawler gets a sponsor with benefactor boxes. Valtay "Neural Enhancer" third-party upgrades.
- **Crawler-killers** get skulls and a Savage box with a PVP coupon per party member (coupon gives boxes and a level for killing a target).
- **Liquids** can no longer be stored in inventory unless in a container (announced near the end).
- **Chat** can be subscribed to by paying viewers; crawlers scatter on the next floor except pre-formed parties.
- **Potions**: stat potions limited to two each; throwing a potion hits mobs; mobs do not get potion sickness.
- **DTs / ghoul system**: Mobs addicted to Rev-Up vitamin shots go through stage 1 (shaking), stage 2 (doubled strength, can't reason), stage 3 (hippo-sized tentacled monster that breaks into Krakaren clones). Ghoul generators powered by soul crystals spawn ghouls whenever non-undead die; overload makes them explode.
- **Gods**: invulnerable until floor 12; involuntary summoning lasts vessel level + god level seconds; gods are "soul armor."
- **Death Challenge**: gambling pop-up (odds 50 to 1) in which a monster and a god fight a crawler.
- **Admins** cannot be attacked; the studio is outside the dungeon (limited inventory in the surface zone).

## 5. Floors, levels and locations

**Overall Tangle look.** Underground rail world: tight rocky tunnels with sparse lights, third-rail electrical hazards, ceramic insulators, cavernous junctions where tracks cross over and under each other. Feels like a Soviet-era Metro merged with industrial hell. Palette: dingy beige plastic, brown cushions, spray-painted Cyrillic, white tile, rust red, ochre, sickly green gore. Saferooms are branded fast food places (Nirula's, Arby's, J.CO Donuts, Hotdog on a Stick, Soda from Costa Rica, Everest, a pho shop).

**Moscow Red Line Car 20 and Car 15 (Janitor's Lair).** Beige molded plastic seats, brown cushions, scorch marks, poles; smells like dead rats. Doorless gangways with accordion fabric; floor panels. Janitor's lair: pitch-dark, humid, bones, brooms, two Jikininki. Gimmicks: ghouls are docile unless you bleed; monsters can't get off at non-transfer stops. Tone: horror-comedy.

**Sirin Station 81 and Mora 82.** Platform packed with hundreds of Dreks (demonic babies) and a hedgehog-ogre Psycho Sticker.

**Transfer Station 83.** White-tile platform, yellow stripe, one bench, steps up to a circular hub: Mace guild, Limp Richard's Sundries, Nirula's (red and white), stairs down to Yellow and Nightmare platforms. The Nightmare platform is twice as long, wider tracks, no third rail, figure-eight route (83, 283, 436, 283, 83).

**The Royal Palace of Princess Donut (personal base).** Airplane-hangar room, concrete floor, industrial kitchen, couch with three screens, Training Room (holographic four-armed whip monster), Crafting Studio (alchemy, sapper, engineering, makeup, armorer tables), Manager door, individual doors with colored name labels. Katia later whitelists others. Cleaner bot, glitter everywhere.

**Yellow Line train.** English graffiti, blue cushions; engine car one is solid metal. Car 5 Employee Break Room (bar, Conductor's Quarters); Car 10 Porters' reward room with suitcases on shelves.

**Banshee Station 116.** Wet rocky caves, slimy walls, four big rooms, no saferoom. Cornets (skinless rabbit monsters) with sound attack; vials scattered. Hidden warehouse room with blue control sigil, a wire cage cart, and two sleeping dwarven automatons (black and silver Transformers-like robots, bulldozer size).

**Station 131 (Yellow).** Ford's Fighting Supplies, longbow guild, "Druids only" guild, Arby's saferoom, elf clothing store, Desperado Club (tiny dive bar with neon sign). Club interior: dance floor with EDM, badger-headed bartender, crocodile bouncers, back offices, Penis Parade and "Bitches" strip rooms, casino with wheel of fortune, roulette (four colors, bones/knives/planets), stairs to the Hunting Grounds and to Larracos.

**Ochre Line Station 149.** Small transit station: pho shop, general store, church-like Club Vanquisher (ram-headed cleric). After the derailment: toppled engine, peeled passenger car, black smoke, small fires; third rail at the platform edge. Enemies: tentacle wolves, flying skunks with butcher knives. Boss: Gore-Gore.

**Mauve Line Station 281 and 283.** J.CO Donuts glass storefront saferoom. Station 283 landing for the Nightmare Express (red-lit tunnel, steam, cowcatcher, tracks 2 stories wide). Food court saferoom at 283 with a Hotdog on a Stick.

**Dismemberment Limited.** Sleek white maglev monorail, 3 cars, glass nose, blood-caked interior; quest "Levi is On the Menu."

**Nightmare Express (steam train).** Jet-black coal engine, 666 in red on the boiler, ~40 cars: open-top livestock cars with chain-link cages (tentacles, fire, narwhal water tank), caboose entrance only. Cab: valves, gauges, two dead drivers, firebox with a demon woman, "Shattering Train Bomb" overpressure. Steam wall, humid hazy tunnel.

**Switching Cavern.** Large round target switches ("Auxiliary Tracks," "Station Repair," "Recycle") and a giant glowing portal.

**Abyss Station 436 and the Abyss.** Bare platform, steep industrial stairs (an hour climb) to a circular catwalk over a mile-wide, thousands-of-feet-deep fiery pit ringed with hundreds of portals; trains burst out and fall into the pit full of burning wrecks; ghouls swarm below; lizard-like Wall Monitors on the walls. Tone: awe plus dread.

**Trainyards (E, C, D, H, M, Q, etc.).** Mile-wide underground yards with washed-out spotlights, turntable the size of a baseball field, watchtowers, fences shaking with festering ghouls, corrugated awnings, service bays, Soviet-brutalist Substation buildings (burning). Later: station mimic city boss as an administrative building with a 300-foot mouth.

**Employee Line and Station 60.** Dark service track free of monsters; station 60 is a cobwebbed empty platform leading to portals for 12 Homeward Bound platforms; no town (false memories).

**Stairwell rooms (24, 36, 48, 72).** Warehouse-sized rocky room, five glowing stairwells in a circle under spotlights, ten exits to train platforms, a hidden trapdoor ladder disguised as rock; the floor etching shows the Syndicate logo side view.

**Station 75.** Hobgoblin/gnoll depot: small flatbed rail carts, graveyard cavern, gnoll armory, hobgoblin repair shop, "The Downward Dog" bar.

**The Noodle.** The in-between escape tunnel; gravity points toward the closest wall like a loop-de-loop.

**Production trailer submarine.** Velvet-lined deluxe trailer with a floor-to-ceiling window onto a sandy sea floor with round plants and flat fish; stage-set studio for "Planet Beautiful."

**Floor 5 preview.** Warehouse with wheels (Land/Sea/Air/Subterranean; Razor Fox Kite City, Dirigible Gnome Wasteland Fortress, Nightgaunt Precipice, Great Dirt Island of the Wind Sirens). The team enters the **Air quadrant**, desert dunes with blowing sand, aiming to storm a castle in the sky.

## 8. Humor, tone and recurring bits

- **Running tone:** gallows humor over genuine tragedy. The satire targets corporate overtime, HR, deadly transit, drug dealing as logistics, and reality-TV spin. Bright moments follow cruel moments.
- **System announcements:** cheerful menace ("Now get out there and kill, kill, kill!"; "Quit sucking."). Item blurbs mock Borant and make pop-culture jokes (Jurassic Park, Alien, Dr. Phil, Steven Seagal).
- **Alarm trap music:** the loud random pop hit is a recurring gag, sometimes saving the day.
- **Donut's all-caps chat**, her imperial voice, negotiation sprees, and "Carl plan" accusations.
- **Mongo** licking up gore, eating things, happy to "clean" floors.
- **Zip-lining Donut**, "Surprise One/Two/Three" combos, "Formation One/Two/Position Three."
- **"Glurp, glurp, motherfucker"** becomes a viewer chant after Carl and the Maestro's embarrassment.
- **Carl's mantra:** "You will not break me. Fuck you all." echoing.
- **Odette, Maestro, Chaco, Frank and Maggie** show recurring villains; the Maestro is repeatedly humiliated.
- **"You can't save them all"** (Mordecai) versus Carl's guilt.
- **Book trades:** Carl bribes bored shopkeepers with Louis L'Amour books.
- **Prime numbers**, the "zomp" color, and Katia's art-history nerd explanations.
- **Recurring cat-owner trauma** about Bea and the litterbox.

## 9. Ideas for the side-scroller game

1. **Train-car Level (horizontal sidescroll):** Each car is a screen with poles you hop around; doors need a hold-to-open mechanic. Palette: beige plastic, brown seats, red LED sign, graffiti. Add the Drek swarm that climbs ceilings.
2. **Protective Shell Rail Dash:** Boss-lite section where Carl casts a stationary shield from a moving train, and the screen scrolls so enemies get smeared. Great for a signature mechanic.
3. **Alarm Trap item:** Place a trap that plays a chiptune version of a random pop hit; enemies are stunned or drawn. Randomized song titles in pop-ups.
4. **Third-rail hazard:** Electrified lines with a glowing yellow floor stripe; enemies get fried if knocked in. Gore-Gore boss: knock him onto the rail as a finisher.
5. **Gore-Gore boss mechanics:** Charge phases, blades, and a 1.5% punch counter that grows (the Grull summon timer). Let the player choose to stop punching or risk the god.
6. **Janitor Jikininki:** Mid-level enemy that ignores you unless you bleed; gives a "do not get hit" stealth segment.
7. **Cornet rooms:** Sound-wave attack that inverts controls or makes the screen wobble ("Queasy"); resistance perk earned after several hits.
8. **Control Sigil:** A glowing blue floor symbol only visible with a special lens; stepping off it triggers a puzzle gate.
9. **Hub base:** Donut's hangar as a hub with upgradable stations (alchemy, sapper, armor) and a Roomba-style cleaner bot.
10. **Katia plow-train set piece:** A fast auto-scroller where a giant Katia is the train front and the player's job is to heal, shoot and avoid wedged corpses. Gore-splatter meter fills the HUD.
11. **Nightmare Express heist:** Dodge-the-cowcatcher cinematic, then a boiler-pressure minigame with Fire Brandy giving valve instructions (turn left, turn right).
12. **Fire Brandy NPC:** A talking face in the boiler door giving advice; a sad ending where she chooses to sacrifice.
13. **Abyss vista:** A huge parallax pit with portals spewing train wrecks; platforms along a rusty catwalk, lizard monsters climbing.
14. **Trainyard E mimic boss:** City-block-sized blob forming a building mouth; player must cut >50% of mass (damage-gated phases) while avoiding the tongue; minion Jibber-Jabbers split when hit.
15. **Grull set piece:** Run-and-hide stealth level using invisibility and smoke curtains while the axe quake shakes the screen; Death Challenge splash screen with betting-odds overlay.
16. **Growler Gary Cart puzzle:** A rail cart that needs two glowing hand-print plates; the gag is the bartender who regenerates, with a darkly funny "one more time" retry loop.
17. **Achievements in the book's voice:** "Choo Choo, Motherfucker" (ride a train), "Martha Stewart" (first crafting), "Locomotive Breath" (derail a train), "Three Cheers for Slaughter" (kill three bosses with one blast).
18. **Item ideas:** Sheol Brick (slow-burning fuel), Banger Spheres (xistera ammo), Jelly Bomb, Fragmenting Potion Ball, Seize the Day Toothpaste (boss damage buff), Beanie summoning toys.
19. **Enemy ecology gag:** Monsters line up at a window to get a vial; players can sabotage this and watch the withdrawal tiers (shakes, rage, tentacle form) as escalating difficulty.
20. **Epilogue wheel spin:** A Wheel of Fortune style level-transition screen that picks the next biome (Land, Sea, Air, Subterranean), ending with a desert-dune doorway into the sky-fortress level.
