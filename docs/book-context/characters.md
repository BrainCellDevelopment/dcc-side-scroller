# Characters (books 1 to 3)

Paraphrased from the three book summaries. Spoilers. "Art cue" lines are the visual details most useful for sprites. Source section references: each `book-0N-*.md` has a full characters section.

## The party

### Carl
- **Who:** Narrator and player character. A 27-year-old ex-Coast Guard marine technician from Seattle (book 1). Crawler #4,122. Gruff, sarcastic, guilt-prone, stubbornly decent. Catchphrase "Goddamnit, Donut"; mantra "You will not break me."
- **Art cue:** 6'3", about 230 lb, muscular. Starts the story in boxers, a leather jacket and his ex-girlfriend's pink Crocs. Over the books: a one-sleeved leather jacket, heart-print "BigBoi" boxers, Nightgaunt cloak, trollskin shirt, spiked kneepads, a magic war gauntlet on his fist, a toe ring. Barefoot with toughened soles, and no pants for most of the story. In book 1 he smokes until his last cigarette.
- **Fighting style:** Bare-knuckle brawler in book 1 (Pugilism, Iron Punch, Powerful Strike). From book 2 he is a Primal Compensated Anarchist: explosives, traps, a jai-alai xistera for throwing. Level 13 at the end of book 1, 27 at the end of book 2, 41 at the end of book 3.
- **Arc:** From survival to protecting strangers (the care-home group), to bargaining with the show's producers, to building a team. Carries the "Carl's Doomsday Scenario" nuke from book 2.

### Princess Donut (the Queen Anne Chonk)
- **Who:** Persian cat that belonged to Carl's ex Bea; becomes sapient after a Legendary Pet Box and an Enhanced Pet Biscuit in book 1. Vain, dramatic, fame-hungry, secretly smart and loyal. Types in ALL CAPS in chat. Calls Carl her manservant. Loves salmon, purple, TV and cameras.
- **Art cue:** Tortoiseshell (black, beige and white) Persian with a flat face, huge fluffy coat and yellow eyes. A tiny jeweled purple tiara and a butterfly charm on her collar that jingles. Later a silver crupper "skirt" armor with a tail slot. Rides on Carl's shoulders and neck. (Our game puts her on his head.)
- **Abilities:** Magic Missile from her eyes, Torch, Puddle Jumper (short teleport), Second Chance, Heal, later Clockwork Triplicate. Charisma-driven charm of non-boss mobs on early floors. Class from book 2: Former Child Actor (picks a themed specialty each floor).

### Mongo
- **Who:** A baby velociraptor (Mongoliensis) adopted at the end of book 1. Screams, bites, eats corpses, loves pet biscuits.
- **Art cue:** In book 1, 7 inches tall with pink downy feathers on a plucked-chicken body, tiny forearms and a serpentine tail. Grows each level: cat-sized, turkey-sized, then pony-sized by book 3 (13 ft long, red and blue feathers, a saddle). Sparking "Fang Caps" and a black tracking collar from book 2.

### Mordecai
- **Who:** Carl and Donut's tutorial guide, an indentured NPC who only wants his freedom; a former golden-eagle-like skyfowl turned "changeling" who changes shape each floor. Fussy, funny, grieving and fond of drink. Keeps his mother's ashes and his brother's photo. Hates Odette, who trained him.
- **Forms, by floor:**
  - Tutorial and Floor 1: a Rat Hooligan, a bearded rat with a black vest, blue pants and sandals.
  - Floor 2: a Bugaboo, a seven-foot black bear-like creature with huge owl eyes.
  - Floor 3: an Incubus (Lv 50), gray skin, devil horns, a ponytail, bat wings, a barbed tail, a tuxedo he hates.
  - Floor 4: a five-foot mud-colored Grulke toad (and he is also put in a seven-day time-out by the show).
- **Role:** Becomes Donut's "manager" in book 2, able to teleport into saferooms but not to fight. His obligation ends after Floor 4, when the system protects him.

### Katia Grim
- **Who:** Shy Icelandic art teacher/professor who is secretly a Doppelganger (shapeshifting clay-like body). Monster Truck Driver class: huge Constitution when moving, a battering-ram Rush. Joins in book 2, a main party tank in book 3.
- **Art cue:** Hand-sculpted face; lumpy at first, later a pale, black-haired woman in her mid 30s. Can wear a black bodysuit and a purple mohawk.

## System, production and media voices
- **The System AI:** Snarky game-show-host voice that narrates achievements, tooltips and boss intros. Has a running foot fetish, mocks Carl constantly, and sometimes does impressions. A corporate female voice announces survivor counts and rules; a stern voice appears with admins.
- **Zev:** Two-foot-tall kua-tin Borant staffer in a bubble helmet and white water suit (1950s astronaut look). Becomes Carl and Donut's PR agent; replaced by **Loita**, a taller, stricter Bloom-party admin, in book 3.
- **Odette:** Host of "Dungeon Crawler After Hours." Rides a floating disc in a costume of a crab-like lower body and a mantis helmet. Former crawler. Gives cynical, useful advice and owns Carl's interviews.
- **Others on camera:** Ripper Wonton (quokka host of Danger Zone), Evo (white hairless Soother director), Chaco the Bard (winged wolf-headed prize host), Princess D'nadia (squid-faced royal sponsor).

## Allies and NPCs
- **Tally, Sebastian, Qwist, Gordo, Wendita (Bopca):** Gnome-like saferoom caretakers who cook and fall under Donut's charm. Tally has a red-veined nose and a paper hat.
- **Meadow Lark crew (book 1):** Eldercare workers and residents from Wenatchee, WA. Brandon An (leader and fixer, lightweight hammer), his brother Chris (near-silent, spear), Yolanda (archer, killed by the Rage Elemental), Imani (quiet, longsword; later a skull-faced healer with monarch wings), Agatha (homeless woman with a shopping cart, pink flamingo and secret alien tech), Mrs. Elle McGibbons (99 and funny; later a tiny blue Frost Maiden). Brandon dies off-screen by book 3.
- **Hekla:** Blond Amazonian shieldmaiden with a repeating crossbow. Starts as an ally, becomes a manipulator, and is killed by Katia in book 3.
- **Daniel Bautista:** Orange tiger-man swashbuckler who leads a group of crawlers.
- **Shopkeepers and fixers:** Quint (half-djinn pharmacist with an opossum head), Pustule (hobgoblin explosives dealer), Clarabelle (crocodile bouncer at the Desperado Club), Limp Richard (mole-man shop owner), Growler Gary (a shade gnoll bartender who regenerates).

## Villains and antagonists
- **Frank Q and Maggie My:** A murderous couple of crawlers (axe, throwing knives, pistol). Frank loses a hand to Carl's trap; later he is a drunk night elf who gives Carl a cursed ring.
- **The Maestro:** A huge orc prince in a hot pink shirt who runs the death-game show "Death Watch." Turns up again in book 3, alive, piloting the war god Grull.
- **Skull Empire royals:** Prince Stalwart, King Rust, Princess Formidable (who sponsors Katia).
- **Tsarina Signet (book 2):** Half-naiad half-elf blood-magic summoner covered in living tattoos.
- **Miss Quill (book 2):** An elderly skyfowl schemer who collects Beanie Babies, mastermind behind the town's plot.
- **Lucia Mar:** A child crawler ranked first, a witch-like inquisitor with two rottweilers who feud with Donut.
- **Eva Sigrid (book 3):** Four-armed cobra-woman enforcer who works for Hekla.
- **The corporations:** Borant (the fish-like kua-tin who run the show), the Bloom party in the kua-tin government, and Valtay (a parasitic rival company whose tech Carl and Agatha carry).

## Using this for the game
- The in-game Mordecai is a purple-robed rat-man. The book version is a bearded rat in a black vest, blue pants and sandals. Worth deciding on purpose.
- Donut should read as dark tortoiseshell with yellow eyes, a purple tiara and a butterfly charm (matches what you asked for in the Donut issue).
- Carl's boxers have hearts, his jacket is leather with one sleeve missing, and he is barefoot.
- Mordecai changes form each floor: a clean way to mark progress between levels.
