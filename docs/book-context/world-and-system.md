# World, System rules and voice (books 1 to 3)

How the dungeon works, plus the tone to write in. Paraphrased from the three book summaries. Spoilers.

## The premise
- A galactic corporation (Borant, run by fish-like kua-tin, under a larger "Syndicate") collapses Earth's buildings and turns the planet into a televised 18-floor "World Dungeon." Survivors are "crawlers"; viewers across the galaxy watch, send gifts and bet.
- Each floor has a timer (5 days on Floor 1, 6 on Floor 2, 8 on Floor 3, 10 on Floor 4). When it ends the floor collapses and anyone left behind dies. Stairwells are the way down; descending too early freezes you until collapse, so the best window is the last few hours.
- There is no respawn. Dead crawlers drop everything except unopened boxes; corpses become lootable after about ten minutes.

## Stats and progression
- Five stats: Strength, Intelligence, Constitution, Dexterity, Charisma (Wisdom is hidden). Intelligence is also mana; Constitution affects healing and potion cooldowns. 3 points per level.
- Skills rise with use (thousands exist: Breathing, Walking, Frogger, Pugilism, Iron Punch, Explosives Handling).
- Race and class are chosen on Floor 3 (before that, everyone is a human with no class). Carl becomes a Primal Compensated Anarchist; Donut a Former Child Actor.
- Donut's Charisma lets her charm non-boss mobs with non-red dots on early floors.

## Loot boxes
- Tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legendary, Celestial. Many types: Adventurer, Pet, Weapon, Apparel, Shoe, Goblin, Boss, Looter, Brawler, Survivor's, and more.
- They can only be opened in a safe zone. Contents are tailored to be funny (Carl never gets pants).
- Book 1's turning point: a Legendary Pet Box plus an Enhanced Pet Biscuit makes Donut sapient and strong. (Our game's second loot box is a Legendary box holding a Legendary Pet Biscuit.)
- Fan boxes (voted on by fans) unlock at huge view counts; sponsors send Benefactor Boxes.

## Safe rooms, guilds and bathrooms
- Safe rooms negate mob attacks and crawler violence. Each has a gimmick and a caretaker (a Bopca gnome who cooks). They close an hour before collapse and the door won't open with mobs outside.
- Tutorial Guilds are personal safe rooms run by your guide.
- Bathroom doors open on tiny silver-toilet closets, the only places viewers cannot see.
- Public peeing outside bathrooms eventually summons a Rage Elemental.
- From Floor 3, saferooms cost gold; from Floor 4 you can buy personal spaces with coupons.

## Bosses and elites
- Six tiers: Neighborhood, Borough, City, Province, Country, Floor. Boss rooms lock, play music and show a VS card.
- Neighborhood bosses drop a map upgrade and a Bronze Boss Box; Borough bosses drop a field guide.
- Floor 3 adds scripted "elites" with plot armor, and quests that trigger them.

## The HUD and the show
- Health bar (top right), mana bar, countdown timer, minimap with fog of war, a ten-slot hotbar, a notification folder. Enemy health bars appear only after damage.
- Viewers, followers, favorites and patrons; leaderboards and bounties appear on Floor 3.
- Side shows: "Dungeon Crawler After Hours" (Odette), "Danger Zone," "Death Watch," "Planet Beautiful."
- Languages are translated; Donut is heard as a squeak by mobs.

## The System voice (for text, popups and achievements)
- A snarky game-show host that insults Carl and jokes about his feet. Item descriptions and monster tooltips are punchlines. Announcements end with "kill, kill, kill."
- Recurring bits: Carl never gets pants or shoes; "Goddamnit, Donut"; Donut's ALL CAPS messages and her fame obsession; she hates torches yet owns hundreds; goblins always carry pineapples; Mongo bites Donut's nose; the mysterious "Ferdinand"; Mordecai is allergic to cats.
- Tone: slapstick plus genuine grief. The jokes never erase the fact that millions died.
- Achievement titles from the books (as examples of the voice): "Crazy Cat Lady," "Why Aren't You Wearing Pants?," "Podophilia," "Boom!," "You Monster!," "Boss Babe," "Wait, Bosses Can Leave Their Rooms?"
- Writing rule of thumb: a short rude title, then one sentence that mocks what the player just did, with a little sympathy hidden in it.

## Items worth remembering (canon, from book 1 onward)
- Carl: Nightgaunt Cloak, Trollskin Shirt, spiked kneepads that reflect damage, a toe ring, a magic war gauntlet that forms a spiked orc-steel fist, white heart-print BigBoi Boxers that cast a protective shell, a pedicure kit that powers up his bare feet, a stolen steam chopper.
- Donut: a jeweled tiara, a butterfly-charm collar, a crupper "skirt," a fur brush.
- Weapons and consumables: goblin dynamite, hobgoblin "pus" explosives, Rev-Up moonshine and "Boom Jugs," Mana Toast, experience cookies.
- Pets: Mongo the raptor (grows each level); Menagerie pet options include a parrot, slime, rat and a tanky meatball with a mohawk.

## Our game vs the System
- Our game already has: loot boxes opened in a safe zone, a safe room after a boss, a snarky hint style, achievements with the System's voice, and heart boxers.
- Not yet represented (a first stats module is being built separately): stats screens, classes and races, the quest tab, viewers and sponsors, the leaderboard, and gold. A good next layer is a viewer or follower counter that ticks up as the player does funny or brave things.
