# Levels and looks (books 1 to 3)

Visual style guide pulled from the three book summaries, floor by floor. Spoilers. For fuller detail and line numbers, see section 5 of each `book-0N-*.md`.

## Tutorial and Level 0 (book 1, mapped to the game)

- **Surface, 2:23 AM, freezing, Seattle.** A rectangle of churned dirt and rocks where a building used to be. Trees, bike racks, a stop sign, a booted (wheel-clamped) moped, and car-shaped dents in the asphalt remain. A distant fire burns. Silent, cold, eerie. Pillars of light mark the entrances. *Game:* matches the barren street, signs and lights, bike racks and moped props.
- **Entrance staircase.** Wide enough for twenty people, ornate wrought iron with fish- and demon-like patterns, descending into warm glowing light. The air gets humid and about 80 F. At the bottom: a marble floor and a thirty-foot arched wooden double door carved like a fish demon.
- **Torch-lit tunnel.** As wide and tall as a two-lane road, with branches. A neon sign "Da Tutorial Guild" points into a dark alley (a trap). A narrow half-lit hall, a too-obvious dark hall, and a lichen-lit brick hall with a plain red door marked as the guild.
- **Goblin Murder Dozer.** A tractor-sized rusted contraption with tank treads, a spiked wheel and steam, chasing the crawlers. In the book it blows up against the corridor wall outside the guild. *Game:* our dozer fight is a bigger version.
- **Tutorial Guild room.** A classroom-sized stone room: fireplace, bed, shelves with photos of bird creatures and an urn, a gray oval carpet, a desk and scattered chairs. Mordecai lives here. *Game:* matches the guildhall props.
- **The hallway to the guild in book 2** is a golden-brick hall with a plush red carpet and a dark star-filled nebula painted on the ceiling, ending at the same door.

## Floor 1 (book 1)

- **Layout.** A huge grid of equal squares bounded by wide "artery" tunnels with torches. Each square is a maze of narrower dark-red brick or stone tunnels lit by green glowing lichen, ceilings under 15 feet. Humid, sometimes smells of oil, sewage and rot. Four neighborhoods per square, each with its own boss lair. Fog of war on the map.
- **Safe rooms are chunks of Earth.** A Polish-style fast-food restaurant with a kids' play structure; a dingy bus-station room; a Taco Bell in Peru; a Waffle House from Alabama. Each has a gimmick (free cookies, a soda machine).
- **Bathrooms.** Doors in the halls open to a tiny closet with a silver toilet, the only place viewers can't see.
- **Neighborhoods.**
  - *Goblin Workshop:* a giant steam engine, six parked Murder Dozers, tool tables, a coal mountain, barrels of "funpowder." Smells of machines and pineapple. Goblins carry pineapple-on-a-stick clubs.
  - *Llama alleys:* lava-spitting Bad Llamas.
  - *The Hoarder's lair:* a round room with 15-foot garbage piles and skull-headed torches.
  - *Rot Stickers:* black ravioli-shaped things on walls and ceilings.
  - *Slimes:* green pig-sized blobs that dissolve gear.
  - *Troglodyte gym:* a 24-hour gym boss lair.
- **Borough boss arena.** A huge circle with a ring road and a drawbridge-like entrance. After the gate shuts, the walls sink to reveal a roller-rink and rave dome: concentric grooves, black-light purple light, neon, lasers, thumping bass. The boss is the Ball of Swine.
- **Surface/trailer sets.** After the boss: a yacht cabin on a calm night ocean with a blue velvet couch and a bar.

## Floor 2 (book 1)

- Same grid as Floor 1 but with **white floors**, **orange-tinted lichen** and **cinderblock walls**, narrower arteries and cracked concrete ground. Grub slime trails, corpses and gore.
- **Zones:** Brindle Grub corridors (worm-like janitors), a brain-jellyfish "Mind Horror" quadrant, a concrete storm-shelter safe room with 50 cots and a mailbox, a hillbilly Clurichaun moonshine quadrant (jugs, a tub, a "Live, Laugh, Love" boss door), a kobold quadrant with a dog-pound boss lair (cages, thrash metal, a pit-fighting ring), and a stairwell room ringed by pupae mounds.
- **Safe rooms:** a dusty fried-chicken restaurant, an industrial kitchen with a free toaster.
- **Bosses:** a Krakaren clone (pyramid-scheme octopus), Ralph the frenzied gerbil.

## Floor 3, the Over City (book 2)

- **Overall.** The first above-ground-looking floor: a medieval theme park under a fake rocky ceiling with a **blood-red sun**. Eight-day timer, day and night cycle. Wooden slat streets with gaps; sulfur smoke rises from below (a volcano story). At night: dark, distant fires, screams, guards gone.
- **Village.** Half-timber shops, pubs and a guildhall; faceless plate-armor guards (Level 75 Swordsmen) who only work by day; waist-high fence between town and ruins.
- **The Ruins.** Mossy crumbling brick with no doors or windows, slumped roofs, collapsing wooden streets over basements, fog at street level, holographic signposts.
- **Safe room pub.** The Belly-Rubbed Pug: a Renaissance-fair tavern with a pug in a top hat on the sign; rooms cost gold now.
- **Grimaldi's Traveling Circus.** A fortified camp of three huge tents with walls of rocks, wood and bones, spiked jacks and barbed wire, executioner-robed crews firing glowing mortars, and a brass steam calliope playing slow polka. The Big Top entrance is a giant clown face you walk through the mouth of; inside, a bloody red carpet, chaotic performers and the boss, a giant pale-green vine with python-like roots. The circus is gone by morning and a stairwell appears.
- **Medium Skyfowl Settlement.** Bigger buildings with open windows for flying eagle-folk, a town-hall tower mall, a warehouse of inactive plate-armor guards (a terracotta army with yellow auras), a magistrate's office full of Beanie Babies, and a meat-locker nest behind it.
- **Desperado Club.** 1920s New York art deco: six stories tall, concrete columns, a sunburst relief, a spinning neon knife and the slogan "So fun it hurts." Inside, a neon dance floor on a separate plane and a Blade Runner feel, a casino and a market.
- **Other sets:** a drained YMCA pool with a bloated two-necked whale boss, a Danger Zone talk-show studio, a small decrepit building hiding the stairwell.

## Floor 4, the Iron Tangle (book 3)

- **Overall.** A planet-sized rail network: a one-way web of colored subway lines plus named loop trains. Tight rocky tunnels with sparse lights, third-rail hazards, ceramic insulators, huge junctions where tracks cross over and under. Soviet Metro feel: dingy beige plastic seats, brown cushions, spray-painted Cyrillic, white tile, rust red, ochre, sickly green gore. Ten-day timer.
- **Transfer stations** (prime numbers) hold saferooms and shops; saferooms are branded fast-food places. Other stations are cave nests. Monsters ride between "every fifth stop" rooms to get a drug fix.
- **Set pieces.**
  - A Moscow-style Red Line car (beige molded seats, scorch marks) and a pitch-dark janitor's lair.
  - Transfer Station 83: white tile, yellow stripe, a circular hub with a guild and a store.
  - The Nightmare Express: a jet-black coal steam train with a red 666, about 40 cars, open-top livestock cars and a firebox with a demon woman.
  - Dismemberment Limited: a sleek white maglev monorail with a glass nose.
  - The Abyss: a mile-wide fiery pit ringed with portals that trains burst out of and fall into.
  - Mile-wide trainyards with turntables, watchtowers and burning Soviet-brutalist substations.
  - Stairwell rooms: a warehouse-sized cave with five glowing stairwells in a circle under spotlights.
- **Bosses and mobs.** An evil transit-corporation satire: a ManTauR engineer, drug-withdrawal ghouls that turn into Krakaren clones, a station mimic with a 300-foot mouth.
- **Floor 5 preview.** A warehouse of wheels leading to Land, Sea, Air and Subterranean quadrants; the party enters the Air quadrant, a desert with a sky fortress of Dirigible Gnomes.

## Ideas for turning these into side-scroller levels

- **Keep a consistent arc.** Each level has a clear palette (see the table), a safe room at the end, a loot box moment and a themed boss.
- **Level 1 as the Floor 1 grid.** Torch-lit brick corridors with green lichen glow; goblin neighborhood rooms; a Hoarder-style boss; end with a roller-rink dome arena (the Ball of Swine) as a boss. Pineapple-on-a-stick goblin clubs are canon, so our pineapple throws fit.
- **Level 2 as Floor 2.** Palette flip to white floors and orange lichen; grub trails; a dog-pound boss with pit-fighting arena.
- **Over City level.** Horizontal wooden-plank streets over gaps with sulfur smoke; day/night as a mechanic; the circus Big Top boss with a clown-mouth entrance.
- **Iron Tangle level.** Side-on train cars as platforms, third-rail hazards, moving trains as level hazards, a boss on a steam train.
- **Safe rooms as set pieces.** Each is an Earth building with a gimmick (fast-food booths, a pub, a storm shelter); use them for checkpoints, shops and loot boxes.
- **Mordecai changes form per floor,** which is an easy way to signal progress.
